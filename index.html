Personal information

Max Verstappen was born on September 30, 1997, in Belgium. He is of Dutch nationality and has made a name for himself as one of the most talented Formula 1 drivers in the world. Max is known for his aggressive driving style and exceptional racing skills.

Education

Max completed his high school education at G.Onvanschool in Belgium in 2015. During his youth, he showed great interest in motorsports and participated in various racing academies, where he received specialized training in advanced racing techniques and performance optimization.
